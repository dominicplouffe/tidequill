How to pick a TV stand for a 77-inch TV

I bought the wrong stand first. It was 60 inches wide, my TV was 67, and the feet hung off the edge like a diving board. Don’t be me.

How wide should the stand be?

At least 67 inches, ideally 70 to 75. A 77-inch screen is about 67 inches wide, and you want a few inches of stand on each side so it doesn’t look like it’s balancing.

Suggested direct answer

Check the foot spacing too. Most 77-inch sets sit on two feet near the edges, and some need a surface 60 inches wide just for the feet. The spec sheet lists it under “stand width.”

How much weight can it hold?

Look for a rated load of 100 pounds or more. Solid hardwood legs are a testament to craftsmanship and steadier than hollow metal tubes.
