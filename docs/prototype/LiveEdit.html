How to pick a TV stand for a 77-inch TV

By Fred Laurent, updated Oct 20

I bought the wrong stand first. It was 60 inches wide, my TV was 67, and the feet hung off the edge like a diving board. Don’t be me.

How wide should the stand be?

At least 67 inches, ideally 70 to 75. A 77-inch screen is about 67 inches wide, and you want a few inches of stand on each side.
